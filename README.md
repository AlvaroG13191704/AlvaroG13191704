## What I build

I work at the intersection of **deep learning, generative AI, and backend engineering**: training neural networks, building retrieval-augmented generation pipelines, and connecting language models to tools and data.

My projects explore both sides of AI engineering: understanding the models and building the systems around them.

- **Models:** neural networks, Transformers, multimodal learning, and knowledge distillation.
- **LLM systems:** RAG, tool calling, multi-agent workflows, and Recursive Language Models.
- **Infrastructure:** Python and Go services, containers, and cloud-native deployment.
